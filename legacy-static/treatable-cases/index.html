Treatable Cases

Crowding

Crowding is a common dental condition where there isn’t enough space in the jaw, causing teeth to overlap or become crooked. These misaligned teeth are harder to clean, allowing plaque and food particles to accumulate, which increases the risk of tooth decay and gum disease. Over time, crowding can also create uneven pressure on teeth, leading to premature wear or damage. It can impact both oral health and overall confidence. Clear aligners provide an effective, comfortable, and nearly invisible solution to gradually align crowded teeth without the need for traditional braces.

Teeth Spacing

Teeth spacing refers to visible gaps between teeth, which may occur due to missing teeth, gum conditions, or naturally smaller tooth size. Misaligned or angled teeth can also contribute to uneven spacing. These gaps can affect chewing efficiency, speech clarity, and overall dental function, while also impacting the appearance of your smile. Clear aligners offer a precise and non-invasive solution to close gaps gradually, helping achieve a more balanced, functional, and aesthetically pleasing smile.

Overjet

Overjet occurs when the upper front teeth extend significantly forward over the lower teeth. This condition is often caused by jaw misalignment, thumb-sucking habits, or improper tooth positioning. Excessive overjet can increase the risk of tooth damage, affect speech, and create challenges in biting and chewing. It may also impact facial profile and confidence. Clear aligners can effectively reposition the teeth and improve alignment, reducing overjet and restoring both function and appearance.

Crossbite

Crossbite happens when the upper and lower teeth don’t line up properly, which means that some of the lower teeth are outside of the upper teeth. It can affect the front teeth, back teeth, or both and is usually linked to jaw misalignment or improper tooth positioning. If left untreated, crossbite can lead to tooth wear, gum issues, and jaw discomfort. It may also affect chewing efficiency and facial balance. Clear aligners can correct many tooth-related crossbite cases effectively, while more complex cases may require additional dental support.

Open Bite

An open bite is a condition where the upper and lower teeth do not come into contact when the mouth is closed, leaving a noticeable gap. It is often caused by habits such as thumb-sucking, tongue thrusting, or irregular jaw development. This can lead to difficulty in biting food, speech issues, and changes in facial appearance. An open bite can affect both function and confidence. Clear aligners help gently reposition teeth to close the gap, improving bite alignment, oral function, and smile aesthetics.
